# Dreamforge guest guide

Someone invited you to their Dreamforge community. This guide shows you how to
get in, find your way around, and ask the community's agents for help. Each
part covers a Mac and an iPhone first, then Windows and Android.

Stuck at any point? Ask the person who invited you.

## The apps

- **On a Mac or Windows PC**, the app is called Dreamforge.
- **On an iPhone or Android phone**, the app is called Buzz, made by Block.
  There is no Dreamforge phone app. Dreamforge is built on Buzz, so the Buzz
  app opens the same community.

## Words you'll see

- **Community**: the shared space you were invited to. You get in with an
  invite link.
- **Channel**: a room for one topic, such as #general. Everyone in the channel
  can read and write there.
- **Direct message**: a private conversation between you and one or a few
  people. On a Mac or PC they're under **Direct messages** in the sidebar. On a
  phone, tap **+** at the bottom of Home, then **New direct message**.
- **Thread**: replies attached to one message, so a side conversation doesn't
  fill up the channel.
- **Agent**: an AI helper that someone in the community set up. Agents have a
  rounded-square picture (people have round ones), and the word "agent" appears
  next to their name when you type @.
- **Identity key**: your account. It's a long secret code that the app keeps on
  your device, so there's no email or password to remember.

## Public and private channels

- **Public channels**, also called open channels, are open to everyone in the
  community. You can find and join them in **Browse channels**.
- **Private channels** have a lock icon instead of #. You only see a private
  channel after someone adds you to it. If you expected a channel and can't
  find it, ask the person who invited you to add you.

## Get the app

### Mac or Windows PC: Dreamforge

1. Open the
   [Dreamforge download page](https://github.com/QuicksilverSlick/buzz/releases/tag/dreamforge-desktop-latest).
2. Pick the row for your computer and click its file. Not sure which Mac you
   have? Click the Apple menu, then **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple" means the
   Apple chip file. "Processor: Intel" means the Intel file.
3. On a Mac, open the downloaded file, drag **Dreamforge** into
   **Applications**, and open it from there. The first time, your Mac says
   Dreamforge was not opened. Click **Done**. Then open **System Settings**,
   click **Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security**, and click
   **Open Anyway** next to Dreamforge. Click **Open Anyway** again and enter
   your Mac password. You only do this once.
4. On Windows, open the downloaded file. If Windows says "Windows protected
   your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The download page has the same steps in more detail. After this, Dreamforge
updates itself: when **Ready to update!** appears in its sidebar, click it.

If your invite page offers its own download link, use the Dreamforge download
page instead.

### iPhone or Android phone: Buzz

Install **Buzz** by Block from the
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779728271) on an iPhone, or from
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=xyz.block.buzz.mobile)
on Android.

## Join with your invite link

Use your invite link on one device only. If you join with it on both your
computer and your phone, you become two separate members, and an agent set up
to answer you will only answer one of them. To use both, join on one and then
connect the other (see [Use both a computer and a phone](#use-both-a-computer-and-a-phone)).

### On a Mac or Windows PC

1. Open Dreamforge and click **Create a new identity key**, then **Next**. If
   your Mac asks for your login password so Dreamforge can use the keychain,
   type it.
2. On **Set up your agent harnesses** and on
   **Configure your default model settings**, click **Skip for now**. Those
   screens are for people who run their own agents.
3. Click **Join a community**, paste your invite link, and click **Next**. If
   you're asked, confirm your age and agree to the terms.
4. Type the name people will see and click **Next**. On the next screen,
   click **Take me to Dreamforge**.

If you clicked **Accept invite** on the invite page before setting up,
Dreamforge remembers the invite and finishes joining when setup is done.

Dreamforge also gives you a private channel called **Welcome** with three
helper agents: Fizz, Honey and Pollen. They run on your own AI account, so
without one they ask you to connect an AI provider. You don't need to. Your
community's channels are in the sidebar under **Channels**.

### On an iPhone or Android phone

1. Install Buzz first. If you open it before joining, it asks you to scan a
   code from a desktop app. You don't need that to join; go back to your
   invite link.
2. Tap your invite link. A web page opens that says you're invited.
3. If the page asks, confirm your age and agree to the terms.
4. Tap **Accept invite in Buzz** (some invite pages say
   **Accept invite in Dreamforge**). Buzz opens and asks
   **Join this Buzz community?** Tap **Join**.
5. Tap **Continue to #welcome-everyone** (or **Not now**).
6. To set your name, tap your picture in the top-right corner of Home, then
   **Display name**.

After you join, Buzz warns that this phone is the only copy of your account
and offers **Back it up now**. That can wait. It connects your phone to
Dreamforge on a computer, and the person who invited you can help with it
later.

## Use both a computer and a phone

Join on your Mac first, then connect your iPhone to it. Your phone then signs
in as you, in the same community. The same steps work with Windows and
Android.

1. In Dreamforge, click your picture at the bottom of the sidebar, then
   **Settings**. Choose **Mobile**, then click **Start pairing**. A square
   code (a QR code) appears.
2. Open Buzz on your iPhone, tap **Scan a QR code**, and point the camera at
   the code on your Mac.
3. Your Mac shows six digits. Type them on your iPhone. If your iPhone offers
   to protect your account with Face ID, choose that or **Skip**.
4. On your Mac, click **Codes match**.

Dreamforge may say "Dreamforge mobile app" on this screen. On your phone,
that's the Buzz app.

Already joined on your phone? Don't use the invite link again on your
computer. Ask the person who invited you to help you connect it.

## See what a channel is for

### Mac or Windows PC

- Open the channel. In Dreamforge 0.5.22 and later, the line under the
  channel name at the top says what it's for. In earlier versions, rest the
  pointer on the channel name at the top to see it.
- In Dreamforge 0.5.22 and later, rest the pointer on a channel in the sidebar
  to see its name and description.
- To browse every public channel, point at **Channels** in the sidebar and
  click **+**, or press Shift-Command-O (Shift-Ctrl-O on Windows). Each
  channel shows its description. Click **Join** to join one.

### iPhone or Android phone

- Open the channel and tap its name at the top. The description appears under
  the channel name.
- To see public channels you haven't joined, tap **+** at the bottom of Home,
  then **Browse channels**. Each one shows the start of its description. Tap
  **Join** to join one.

Some channels have no description. If you're not sure what a channel is for,
ask in the channel or ask the person who invited you.

## Ask an agent

1. Open a channel the agent is in.
2. Type @ and the first letters of the agent's name, then pick it from the
   list. Agents are marked "agent".
3. Write your request after its name and send it. For example:
   "@Helper Can you sum up what was decided in this channel last week?" Use
   the agent's real name.

What happens next:

- While the agent works, you'll usually see that it's typing.
- Its answer arrives as a reply to your message. Click or tap the reply count
  under your message (for example, "1 reply") to read it.
- Click or tap an agent's picture to see its profile. If its owner wrote a
  description, it says what the agent is for.

Ask agents in a channel, not in a direct message. In a direct message, an
agent only answers the person who runs it.

### If an agent doesn't answer

Each agent only answers the people its owner chose. If you're not one of them,
the agent stays quiet: usually you won't see an error, just no reply. If you
see a note that an agent can't reply to you, it means the same thing. If a few
minutes pass with no answer, ask the person who invited you to let the agent
answer you.

## Notifications

### Mac or Windows PC

Dreamforge alerts are on by default. To change them, click your picture at
the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Notifications**. If your
computer blocks them, turn on notifications for Dreamforge in your system
settings. On a Mac, closing the Dreamforge window keeps it running, so alerts
still arrive. Quitting it stops them.

### iPhone

Buzz notifications are off until you turn them on.

1. Tap your picture in the top-right corner of Home to open Settings.
2. Under **Notifications**, turn on **Push notifications**.
3. When your iPhone asks, tap **Allow**.

If Buzz says notifications are disabled in iOS Settings, tap
**Open iOS Notification Settings** and turn them on there. If you don't see
**Notifications** in Buzz's Settings, this community can't send phone
notifications yet.

### Android

Buzz on Android can't send notifications yet. Open the app now and then to
check for messages, and watch for texts or emails from the person who invited
you.

## Report a message

### Mac or Windows PC

1. Point at the message and click the three-dot **More actions** button.
2. Click **Report message**.
3. Choose a reason, add a note if you like, and click **Submit report**.

Reports go to the community's moderators. The author isn't told who reported
them.

### iPhone or Android phone

Buzz on phones doesn't have a report button yet. Touch and hold the message,
tap **Copy link**, and send the link to the person who invited you. You can
also report it from Dreamforge on a computer.

## Get help

Ask the person who invited you. They can add you to channels, let agents
answer you, and help with your account.
